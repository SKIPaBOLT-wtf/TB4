and revocations retain their exact meanings. Original A022 three-scope fixtures
declare those three explicitly; a growing enum cannot silently reinterpret them.
Separate four-scope and authority-only fixtures cover opt-in new metadata.
No existing binding gains this purpose, no installed profile is rewritten or
re-enrolled, and no native key is copied. Original closed images/Setup choices
already bound unique purposes by the closed enum; their schema remains unchanged.
An older binary must refuse an unknown new value, never infer compatibility.

Public capability/use results keep only closed outcomes/booleans. FETCHER
summaries still show exactly FETCHER_STATUS and FETCHER_START. Native private
restart preserves identity, selected references, complete image/history and
original external key; late key/permission/expiry/revocation losses refuse use.
Unknown callbacks close held handles, keep profile/history and do not retry.
